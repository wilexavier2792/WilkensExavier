Redundant Power Supply Unit — Removal Guide

Power Supply Unit Components

  • 1. 4-Pin Power Connector
  • 2. 6-Pin Power Connector
  • 3. AC Power Cable Port
  • 4. PSU Fan Unit

Step 1: Disconnect Power Connectors

  • After removing the screws mounting the PSU, disconnect the 6-pin connector from the motherboard.
  • Remove 6-pin ground connector from motherboard.

Step 2: Remove PSU from Case

  • Disconnect the 4-pin connector from the motherboard near the CPU fan.
  • Slide and remove the PSU from the case.
